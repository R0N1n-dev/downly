Satoshi and Cabinet Grotesk are Fontshare fonts and are not on npm, so they are not bundled.
To use them, download them free from https://www.fontshare.com and drop the variable web fonts here:

  Satoshi-Variable.woff2
  CabinetGrotesk-Variable.woff2

Settings > Font then lists them as available. Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit and Geist are bundled.
